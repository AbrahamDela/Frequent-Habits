Frequent Habits — Private, Beautiful & Deep Habit Tracking

Often, you have to make a hard choice when looking for a habit tracker: either choose a visually stunning app trapped behind expensive paywalls and aggressive data harvesting, or settle for privacy-friendly open-source apps that feel visually basic. Frequent Habits bridges that gap completely.

It combines an ultra-modern interface, rich audio-visual feedback, and deep behavioral analytics with 100% offline privacy and zero cloud dependencies.

✨ Key Features & Highlights

📊 Deep Analytics & Time Views
* Interactive Donut Charts: Instantly see your exact breakdown of Completed vs. Failed vs. Pending vs. Paused habits.
* Score & Volume Trends: Track your completion rate (100-point scoring system) and cumulative progress (kilometers, pages, liters) across Weekly, Monthly, and Yearly views.
* Yearly Heatmaps & Weekday Frequency: Spot activity patterns and consistency triggers over long horizons.

🏆 Gamification & Extended Milestones
* Scale Up to 1,000: Milestone achievements for Perfect Days and Total Completions now stretch up to 1,000!
* Diamond Streaks & Trophies: Unlock exclusive diamond series and celebrate milestones with full-screen confetti.
* Custom Self-Rewards: Set personalized real-world rewards for reaching key goals.

📅 Interactive Reviews & Time Capsules
* Performance Reflection: Generate automatic Monthly and Yearly reviews highlighting top streaks and areas for improvement.
* Time Capsule for Your Future Self: Attach notes and memories inside your reviews, locked until future milestone dates.

💡 Algorithmic Smart Insights
* Behavior Pattern Recognition: Get automated, data-driven facts about your routines to boost accountability and understand your habits better.

🎨 App-Wide Customization & Material 3
* Themes & Accent Colors: Toggle effortlessly between Light and Dark mode, paired with custom accent colors.
* Modern UI: Smooth slide-in bottom sheets, clean navigation, and cohesive design across all screens.

🎧 In-App Focus & Audio Timer
* Timed or session-based habits (meditation, reading, deep work) paired with relaxing ambient soundscapes and instant completion chimes.

📱 Redesigned Homescreen Widget
* Check off daily tasks directly from your homescreen with zero latency and an automatic midnight reset.

🔄 Data Ownership & Easy Migration
* 1-Click Import Suite: Seamlessly import backups from other habit trackers (like Loop Habit Tracker) or custom CSV files.
* Local JSON Backups: Export and restore your data anytime using Android's Storage Access Framework (SAF).

🌐 Multilingual Support
* 100% localized out-of-the-box in English, German, Georgian, and Simplified Chinese.

🔒 100% Offline & Private
* No ads, no user tracking, no mandatory accounts, and zero cloud lock-in. Your personal data stays strictly on your device.
